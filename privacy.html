Privacy

Company identity

Website data

This basic website does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors. It does not include customer accounts, payment processing, advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or a web contact form.

Email communications

When someone contacts Zach Ecom by email, the information they provide is used to read and respond to the message and for normal business recordkeeping.

Google Ads API data

The company is developing an internal ecommerce demand-research and Google Ads planning tool for use only by authorized company personnel.

The tool may access Google Ads API keyword-planning services for Google Ads accounts owned or managed by the company. Information may include keyword ideas, historical keyword metrics, search-demand information, and other keyword-planning results.

How Google Ads data is used

Google Ads API data is used only for internal ecommerce research, category and product-demand analysis, keyword organization, and advertising planning for the company’s own Google Ads activities.

The initial tool is read-only and research-focused. It does not create, modify, pause, or delete Google Ads campaigns, advertisements, accounts, conversions, audiences, or remarketing data.

Data storage and access

Research results may be stored in company-controlled local files or other company-controlled storage for internal analysis. Access is restricted to authorized company personnel and retained only as long as reasonably needed for internal business purposes.

Data sharing

Google Ads API data and internally generated research results are not sold, licensed, or provided to external clients, the general public, or unrelated third parties.

Credentials and security

Google Ads developer-token information, OAuth credentials, client secrets, refresh tokens, and other authentication credentials are kept private and are not displayed on this website or provided to external users.
