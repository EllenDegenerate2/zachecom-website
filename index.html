Zach Ecom

Zach Ecom is the application name. It is an internal ecommerce demand-research and Google Ads planning application operated by 1000138257 ONTARIO INC.

The application is used only by authorized company personnel to research product and category demand, generate keyword ideas, review historical keyword metrics, organize research results, and prepare advertising-planning inputs for the company’s own Google Ads activities.

Purpose of the Zach Ecom application

Zach Ecom helps 1000138257 ONTARIO INC. perform structured internal ecommerce research before making advertising decisions.

The application supports category and product-demand analysis by collecting and organizing Google Ads keyword-planning information for manual company review.

Zach Ecom is not a public service, customer platform, client-facing advertising product, or third-party Google Ads management tool.

The application is not sold, licensed, or provided to external users, clients, the general public, or unrelated third parties.

Google Ads API functionality

Zach Ecom uses Google Ads API Keyword Planning Services to generate keyword ideas and retrieve historical keyword metrics related to ecommerce categories, products, and commercial search terms.

This Google Ads data is requested so authorized company personnel can evaluate search demand, organize keyword branches, compare product opportunities, and prepare future Google Ads planning inputs.

Read-only internal use

The initial application is read-only and research-focused.

It does not create, modify, pause, or delete Google Ads campaigns, advertisements, accounts, conversions, audiences, or remarketing data.

Access is restricted to authorized company personnel. OAuth credentials, developer-token information, and Google Ads data are not exposed through this public website.

Why Zach Ecom requests Google Ads data

Zach Ecom requests Google Ads keyword-planning data only to support internal ecommerce demand research and advertising planning.

The information may be used to identify relevant keyword ideas, review historical demand patterns, organize research categories, and prepare manual planning inputs for Google Ads accounts owned or managed by 1000138257 ONTARIO INC.
