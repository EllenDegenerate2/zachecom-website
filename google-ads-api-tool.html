Zach Ecom Google Ads API Tool

Company identity and domain relationship

1000138257 ONTARIO INC. is the legal entity operating the tool. Zach Ecom is the corporation’s public-facing brand and the name of its internal application.

The corporation uses zachecom.com and email addresses ending in @zachecom.com for the Zach Ecom brand, the internal application, and related business communications.

Business model

1000138257 ONTARIO INC. conducts internal ecommerce demand research and advertising planning for its own business activities and Google Ads accounts. The company does not provide Google Ads API access or campaign-management services to external clients.

Tool access and intended users

The tool is used only by authorized company personnel. It is not a public SaaS product, customer portal, agency platform, or third-party advertising tool. There is no public login or external user access.

Tool purpose

The tool supports structured ecommerce demand research and Google Ads planning. A user enters a category, product term, or keyword seed. The tool requests keyword ideas and historical keyword metrics, organizes the response into research branches, and exports structured results for manual company review.

Google Ads API services used

  • KeywordPlanIdeaService.GenerateKeywordIdeas
  • KeywordPlanIdeaService.GenerateKeywordHistoricalMetrics

The tool is limited to Keyword Planning Services described in the Basic Access application.

Functionality not provided

  • No account creation or management
  • No campaign creation or management
  • No ad creation, update, pause, or deletion
  • No conversion tracking or remarketing uploads
  • No client-facing or public reporting service

Tool design and data flow

  1. Authorized user enters a category, product term, or keyword seed.
  2. The local internal application authenticates through OAuth 2.0.
  3. The application calls Google Ads API Keyword Planning Services.
  4. Keyword ideas and historical metrics are returned.
  5. Results are organized into demand-research branches.
  6. Structured results may be exported to local CSV or spreadsheet files for manual analysis.

OAuth credentials, the developer token, and account access information remain private and are not exposed on this website.

Planned internal interface mock-up

1. Research input

Category or seed keyword, target country, and language.

2. Keyword results

Keyword ideas, historical demand metrics, and research branches.

3. Manual review

Authorized personnel inspect relevance and organize category coverage.

4. Structured export

Approved research data is exported for internal planning and analysis.

Data use, storage, and sharing

Google Ads keyword-planning data is used only for internal ecommerce research and advertising planning. Results may be stored in company-controlled local files. Google Ads data, credentials, and developer-token information are not sold, licensed, or shared with clients, the general public, or unrelated third parties.
